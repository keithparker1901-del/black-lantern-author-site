Site information

Privacy

A plain-language summary of how this author website handles visitor and subscriber information.

Mailing-list information

Names and email addresses submitted through the Lantern Road form are sent securely to Resend Contacts. They are not stored in the public GitHub repository and are not sold to advertisers.

Subscribers may unsubscribe through the signed link included in each mailing or by writing to keith@rkeithparkerbooks.com.

Private visitor reporting

The website records page visits and reader actions such as Amazon, Goodreads, BookBub, Facebook, email, and reader-guide clicks in the private Vercel project logs. Common search, social-preview, monitoring, and headless-browser agents are excluded.

The site creates a random anonymous browser identifier in local storage so repeat visits can be distinguished from new visitors. It does not place a reader’s name or email address in visitor records. Browsers with Do Not Track enabled are excluded. The author can also exclude the browser used to maintain the site.

Advertising

The author website does not sell visitor information to advertisers and does not expose private subscriber or visitor records publicly.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to keith@rkeithparkerbooks.com.
