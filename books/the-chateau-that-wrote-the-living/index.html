The next threshold

Ledger-law and written lives

The château does not imprison its guests with chains. It revises them. A hesitation becomes cowardice in the margin. A private grief becomes ingratitude in the official account. A life can be made orderly enough that no living person recognizes it.

Cael enters carrying the one object the château cannot comfortably annotate: a witness light that asks who benefited from the revision.
