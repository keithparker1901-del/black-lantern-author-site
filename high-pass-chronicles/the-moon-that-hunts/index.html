The Moon That Hunts

New to High Pass? Start here.

The dark does not need to break the door.
It only needs to sound like someone you love.

Dogs are killed first.Bells carry lies.Names spoken after dark become bait.

When Hollin Varse staggers into Firwheel Post bloodied and clutching his dead dog's collar, seasoned ranger Edrin Ashvale follows him into Mourning Vale - a scattered high-country community where old warnings are becoming rules again. Collared creatures test thresholds, familiar voices call from the dark, and the same hooked count-mark appears on doors and posts. Whatever is hunting the Vale is not feeding at random. It is testing. It is counting. It is teaching.

A complete story. A larger road.

New to High Pass? This novel stands alone.

The Moon That Hunts is a complete standalone story set in the larger High Pass world. You do not need to read Books One through Four first.

The High Pass Chronicles share a world of haunted roads, remembered laws, witness, burden, and the human cost of survival. Each novel has its own wrong, supernatural system, cast, and resolution.

From Chapter One - The Man at the Door

The road reaches Firwheel Post.

The room held the kind of warmth that only came after a hard road and enough weather to make a hearth feel earned.

Firwheel Post held it well. The great breathing hearth at the center of the common room drew and gave by turns, its iron throat fed by the wheel beyond the wall, so that the fire did not merely burn; it labored.

Edrin Ashvale sat with one shoulder a little away from the heat. Ash-Quiet leaned within reach against the wall, wrapped in shadow except where the polished curve of the ash wood caught a low shine from the fire.
