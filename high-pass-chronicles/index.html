The laws of the High Pass

Atmosphere is never decoration.

⌁

The land remembers

Roads, bridges, houses, and mountain passes retain what people would rather forget.

≡

Objects carry proof

Ledgers, lamps, nails, bells, stones, doors, and masks turn hidden wrongdoing into evidence.

✦

Mercy must be proven

Private goodness is not enough. Repair and release must become visible in public practice.

◇

Power always costs

Every supernatural rule has a limit, and every choice leaves something behind.
