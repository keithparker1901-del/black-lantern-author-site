Review-copy policy

Independent reviews only

A complimentary review copy is offered for consideration, not in exchange for a review. A review is never required, and no positive rating, favorable wording, publication deadline, or particular platform is required as a condition of receiving a copy.

Honest reactions are welcome whether positive, mixed, critical, or a decision not to finish. R. Keith Parker Books does not ask reviewers to alter or suppress an unfavorable review.

Who may request

Reviewers & professional readers

Requests are welcome from book bloggers, independent reviewers, librarians, booksellers, journalists, podcasters, educators, BookTok/BookTube/Bookstagram creators, and other readers with a genuine public reviewing practice or professional interest in the genre.

When requesting, please include a link to the place where you normally discuss or review books and a brief note about your interest in the title.
