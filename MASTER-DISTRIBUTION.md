## Standardization rules

1. Detailed public book/series pages should use one consistent purchase vocabulary: `Kindle`, `Paperback`, `Hardcover`, and `Buy Paperback Direct` where a verified direct link exists.
2. High-level catalog cards may use `Buy on Amazon` when one canonical Amazon landing page is intentionally serving as the reader entry point, but detailed pages must expose verified formats individually.
3. Never invent or infer an ISBN. Publish only values verified from KDP, Ingram/Lightning Source, the book file, or another authoritative bibliographic record.
4. Use direct Ingram purchase links only after verifying the title and ISBN against the publisher email or account record.
5. The Press & Trade page may show bibliographic metadata and professional ordering information without changing the live Book One conversion layout.
6. GA4 retailer tracking should treat retailer_click as a general retailer event and amazon_click/ingram_click as channel-specific events; do not add overlapping events together when reporting conversions.
