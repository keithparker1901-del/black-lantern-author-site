# ARC & Reviewer Outreach Pipeline — Book One

Primary title: THE MANOR THAT DRANK THE ROAD
Goal: build genuine external social proof without manipulating reviews.

## Milestones

- Stage 1: 5 genuine ratings/reviews
- Stage 2: 10 genuine ratings/reviews
- Stage 3: 25 genuine ratings/reviews

Report Amazon and Goodreads separately. Do not add overlapping retailer events or duplicate review counts together.

## Ethical operating rules

1. Offer a complimentary review copy; never offer money, gifts, refunds, discounts, contest entries, future benefits, or reciprocal reviews in exchange for a review.
2. A review is never required as a condition of receiving a review copy.
3. Never request a positive review or a specific star rating.
4. Never ask a reviewer to change, remove, delay, or suppress an unfavorable review.
5. Avoid asking close friends, relatives, employees, business associates, or anyone with a direct personal/financial relationship to post Amazon reviews.
6. Keep outreach individualized and genre-matched; no indiscriminate bulk spam.
7. Track who received a copy and whether they chose to review, but do not pressure non-reviewers.
8. Preserve negative and mixed reviews as legitimate evidence of independent readership.

## Channel A — BookSirens pilot

Recommended first paid test because it is low-commitment and designed around honest ARC reviews.

Current public pricing checked September 2026:
- Promote: $10 per ARC + $2 per reader BookSirens finds.
- Invited readers are not billed as paid-discovery readers.
- Reader cap can be set to limit spend.
- BookSirens states a site-wide review rate around 75%, but this is not a guarantee for any individual book.

Recommended pilot if approved by the author:
- Cap at 8–10 BookSirens-discovered readers.
- Maximum discovery cost at 10 readers: about $30 total under current public pricing.
- Turn on reader-request approval before launch so genre fit and prior review activity can be checked.
- Preferred public review destinations: Goodreads and Amazon, where permitted by each platform's rules.

Status: NOT PURCHASED. Requires explicit author approval before spending money or creating a paid campaign.

## Channel D — Professional editorial proof

Reedsy Discovery is not a replacement for reader reviews. It is a later trade-credibility layer.

Current public price checked September 2026: $299 per completed editorial review. A professional review can be quoted on the website, retailer pages, press materials, and advertising if received and published appropriately.

Recommended timing: after the first reader-review milestone, not before.

## What not to do yet

- Do not spend $575 on a NetGalley six-month listing while the catalog is still at zero/near-zero reader reviews; current economics are disproportionate to this stage.
- Do not buy high-cost editorial reviews merely to manufacture a star rating.
- Do not run broad giveaways with review language that implies winners owe a review.
- Do not mass-message dozens of bloggers.
- Do not spread the first review campaign across all 16 books. Concentrate on Book One.

## Tracking fields

For every reviewer/outlet, record:
- Name/outlet
- URL
- Genre fit
- Review policy checked date
- Contact method
- Date pitched
- Response
- ARC sent date
- Review expected? (never required)
- Review link(s)
- Permission to quote review? if separately obtained
- Follow-up date (one polite follow-up maximum unless invited)

## Decision point

After 10 independent readers have received/claimed a review copy, pause new acquisition long enough to measure:
- completion rate
- review rate
- Goodreads reviews/ratings
- Amazon reviews/ratings
- recurring reader interest
- newsletter opt-ins, if separately and voluntarily obtained

Then decide whether to scale to 25 readers or change targeting.
