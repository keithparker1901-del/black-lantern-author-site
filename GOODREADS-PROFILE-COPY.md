## About the author — paste-ready

R. Keith Parker is a Tennessee author of old-world Gothic fantasy and the author of The Black Lantern Cycle, The High Pass Chronicles, and The Black Salt Cycle.

A social studies teacher, former coach, and farmer from the mountains of Tennessee, he writes haunted roads, old houses, fae courts, dragons, forgotten laws, memory, witness, debt, and the human cost of supernatural systems people obey because they have forgotten who first demanded them.

New readers can begin with THE MANOR THAT DRANK THE ROAD, Book One of The Black Lantern Cycle — a Gothic fantasy of haunted roads, guest-law, forgotten names, and the Black Lantern.

## Goodreads blog post — recommended next post

Title: Where to Begin with R. Keith Parker

If you are new to my books, begin with THE MANOR THAT DRANK THE ROAD, Book One of The Black Lantern Cycle.

It is an old-world Gothic fantasy of haunted roads, guest-law, forgotten names, dangerous hospitality, and the Black Lantern — a witness-light carried into places where custom has become law and law has forgotten who paid its price.

From there, THE VALLEY THAT LAUGHED AT THE LANTERN carries the story into Gleann Sí, a Celtic-fae valley of stolen grief, dangerous beauty, thorn-law, and laughter without truth.

Readers who want a different road can begin THE HIGH PASS CHRONICLES with THE PASS THAT COLLECTS, or enter THE BLACK SALT CYCLE with THE SALT ROAD THAT FED THE DRAGON, an old-world Gothic fantasy of dragons, ancient trade roads, salt, contracts, witness, and unpaid debt.

Old roads. Haunted houses. Forgotten laws. Dark mercy.
