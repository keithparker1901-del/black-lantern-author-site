The laws beneath the road

Discovery is never neutral.

Ⅰ

The land keeps accounts

Roads, drowned foundations, salt beds, and trade routes preserve obligations long after the people who made them are gone.

Ⅱ

Objects become proof

Relics are not treasure alone. Custody, provenance, and possession can expose the wrong that history tried to bury.

Ⅲ

Proof awakens claims

To uncover evidence is to enter the law surrounding it. Knowledge narrows the expedition's safe return.

Ⅳ

Authority survives ruin

A city can die while its rules remain alive—and older powers may still expect tribute, obedience, or judgment.

What defines Black Salt

Adventure under pressure. Gothic consequence underneath.

Black Salt widens the world beyond the haunted road into expeditions, contested ruins, buried peoples, relic custody, and the dangerous question of who owns the truth once it is recovered.

Expedition pressureMapped geography, limited routes, and a return journey that grows less certain with every discovery.
Proof-lawEvidence changes power. What the party carries can matter as much as where they travel.
Buried authorityAncient systems are not scenery. They still recognize debts, witnesses, trespass, and possession.
Gothic consequenceWonder and discovery always arrive with moral cost.
