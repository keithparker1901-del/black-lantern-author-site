About the author

History, rural life, and the power of place.

R. Keith Parker is a Tennessee social studies teacher, former coach, and farmer. His fiction blends Gothic atmosphere, supernatural law, perilous journeys, and classic fantasy adventure.

He lives in the mountains of Tennessee with his wife, Lisa C. Parker. He is the father of two and grandfather of three.
