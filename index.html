About the author

R. Keith Parker

R. Keith Parker writes old-world Gothic fantasy shaped by haunted landscapes, ancient memory, dangerous laws, and the cost of survival. He is the author of The Black Lantern Cycle, The High Pass Chronicles, and The Black Salt Cycle.

A social studies teacher, former coach, and farmer from the mountains of Tennessee, Parker brings history, endurance, rural life, and the weight of place into stories where roads remember the missing and kindness may hide a debt.
