Long biography

About the author

R. Keith Parker writes old-world Gothic fantasy shaped by haunted landscapes, ancient memory, dangerous laws, and the cost of survival. He is the author of The Black Lantern Cycle, The High Pass Chronicles, and The Black Salt Cycle, interconnected bodies of work built around witness, consequence, debt, endurance, and the unsettling power of place.

A social studies teacher, former coach, and farmer from the mountains of Tennessee, Parker brings a deep sense of history, rural life, physical endurance, and inherited memory to his fiction. His stories move through haunted roads, isolated communities, old customs, and supernatural systems that demand to be examined rather than merely escaped.

His flagship Black Lantern novels follow Cael Veyr, a road-worn witness carrying a light whose purpose is not simply to destroy darkness, but to reveal what the darkness has agreed to call necessary—and who was made to pay for it.
